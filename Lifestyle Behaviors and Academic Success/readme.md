# Lifestyle Behaviors & Academic Success

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes the relationship between student lifestyle behaviors
and academic success to identify patterns, key contributing factors, and
actionable insights.

The analysis follows a structured, end-to-end data analytics approach,
starting with problem definition and an issue tree, followed by data
analysis in Excel and visualization and storytelling using Tableau.

### Project Workflow

1. **Problem Statement**
2. **Issue Tree**
3. **Data Exploration & Analysis**
4. **Identify Key Patterns**
5. **Tableau Visualization**
6. **Insights & Recommendations**

## 🎯 Business Problem

Academic success can be influenced by a combination of behavioral,
academic, and lifestyle factors. Understanding these relationships can
help educational stakeholders identify potential areas for intervention
and provide targeted support to students.

The objective of this analysis is to explore student lifestyle and
behavioral data and determine which factors are associated with
academic performance.

## 📈 Excel Data Analysis

The dataset was analyzed using Microsoft Excel to explore the
distribution of variables, identify relationships between lifestyle
behaviors and academic performance, and uncover key patterns in the data.

### 🔹 Descriptive Statistical Analysis

Performed descriptive statistical analysis to understand the
distribution and characteristics of the key variables, including:

- Mean
- Median
- Standard deviation
- Minimum and maximum values
- Quartiles
- Range

### 🔹 Distribution Analysis

Used **histograms** to examine the distribution of key variables and
identify patterns, variability, and potential outliers.

### 🔹 Relationship Analysis

Used **scatter plots** to visually examine relationships between
lifestyle factors and academic performance.

Examples include:

- Study time vs. academic performance
- Sleep duration vs. academic performance
- Attendance vs. academic performance

### 🔹 Correlation Analysis

Developed a **correlation heatmap** to measure the strength and
direction of relationships between key lifestyle and academic variables.

The analysis helped identify variables with stronger positive or
negative relationships with academic performance.

### 🔹 Regression Analysis

Performed **regression analysis** to further evaluate the relationship
between selected lifestyle factors and academic performance.

The regression analysis was used to assess:

- Direction of relationships
- Strength of relationships
- Statistical significance
- Model fit
- Potential predictive factors

## 📊 Tableau Visualization

The results of the Excel analysis were transformed into Tableau
visualizations to communicate key findings and make the analysis easier
for stakeholders to interpret.

The Tableau analysis focuses on:

- Academic performance trends
- Lifestyle behavior patterns
- Relationships between lifestyle factors and academic outcomes
- Comparison of student groups
- Key factors associated with academic success
