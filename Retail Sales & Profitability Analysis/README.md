# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail transaction data to identify profitability drivers, evaluate pricing and discount strategies, and understand regional and customer purchasing patterns using Python and Tableau.

## Business Problem
Retail businesses need to understand how revenue, costs, discounts, quantity, and unit prices influence profitability. This project uses data analysis and statistical modeling to identify business opportunities and support data-driven decisions.

## Objectives
- Analyze revenue, profit, and profit margin.
- Identify relationships between business variables.
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Compare product category and regional performance.
- Analyze customer payment methods.
- Apply correlation and regression analysis.
- Build interactive Tableau dashboards.

## Dataset
The dataset contains 10,000+ retail transaction records and fields such as Quantity, Unit Price, Discount Rate, Cost, Revenue, Profit, Product Category, Region, Payment Method, and Order ID.

## Key Findings
- Revenue has a strong positive relationship with profit.
- Discount rate shows a negative association with profit.
- Electronics has the highest displayed category profit.
- The North region has the highest displayed revenue.
- Several payment methods have comparable transaction counts.

## Business Recommendations
- Use targeted discounts to protect profit margins.
- Investigate the drivers of high-performing product categories.
- Compare regional performance to improve resource allocation.
- Use customer purchasing patterns to inform customer experience initiatives.
- Validate regression results before using them for prediction or causal claims.

This project combines data cleaning, exploratory analysis, statistical modeling, and Tableau visualization to identify retail profitability patterns and develop data-driven business recommendations.
